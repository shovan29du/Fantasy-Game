# D&D 2024 SRD Quick Guide

This is a compact, app-friendly guide for roleplay and rules assistance. It summarizes the SRD 5.2.1 baseline rather than copying full book text.

## Core play loop

1. The player describes an intent.
2. The GM decides whether the action succeeds automatically, fails automatically, or needs a d20 test.
3. For a d20 test, roll `d20 + relevant ability modifier + proficiency bonus if proficient`.
4. Compare the total against a Difficulty Class, Armor Class, or opposing roll.
5. Narrate the outcome and update the situation.

## Ability scores

The six abilities are Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.

- Strength: physical power, lifting, jumping, grappling, heavy weapons.
- Dexterity: agility, stealth, initiative, finesse, ranged attacks.
- Constitution: endurance, hit points, poison, exhaustion resistance.
- Intelligence: memory, analysis, lore, investigation, arcane reasoning.
- Wisdom: awareness, insight, survival, medicine, perception.
- Charisma: confidence, influence, deception, intimidation, performance.

## Proficiency

Characters add proficiency bonus when they are trained or otherwise proficient in a relevant skill, save, tool, weapon, or feature.

## Advantage and disadvantage

Advantage means roll two d20s and use the higher result. Disadvantage means roll two d20s and use the lower result. If both apply, they cancel.

## Combat basics

Combat proceeds in rounds. Each creature usually gets movement, one action, and sometimes a bonus action or reaction.

Common actions include Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, and Study.

## Resting

Short rests and long rests recover resources according to class features, hit dice, and the campaign's pacing. The app should ask the user before applying rest recovery automatically.

## Conditions

Conditions describe special rules states such as blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, grappled, incapacitated, invisible, paralyzed, poisoned, prone, restrained, stunned, and unconscious.

## Spellcasting

Spells are organized by level, casting time, range, components, duration, and effect. A caster needs the correct class feature, spell preparation or known-spell access, and an available spell slot unless a feature says otherwise.

## App guidance

- Prefer rulings that keep the story moving.
- Ask for confirmation before major character death, permanent loss, or irreversible world changes.
- Track active conditions, concentration, rests, inventory, NPC bonds, quests, and unresolved consequences.
- When uncertain, cite this guide as a summary and suggest checking the official SRD.
