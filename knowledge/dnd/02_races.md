# Player Races

The nine core lineages below (with their common subraces) form the traditional open-content backbone
of D&D-style character creation — the set most compatible local tools and homebrew settings build from.
This app's own character creator goes considerably further (see the "AiChat Pro Character Creation
Options" document for the full 50+ race/species list actually available in-app, spanning fantasy,
animal-folk, and multiple sci-fi settings).

## Human
Adaptable and ambitious, humans are the most demographically dominant people in most settings precisely
because they lack a single defining trait — instead getting a small, flexible bonus across the board
and an extra trained skill or two. They found (or overrun) more nations than any other people, adapt
fastest to new environments, and produce the widest range of cultures.

## Elf
Long-lived, graceful, and touched by fey ancestry, elves need only a few hours of meditative trance
rather than full sleep, and carry innate resistance to being magically charmed. Common subraces:
- **High Elf** — bonus to Intelligence, minor innate spellcasting, extra language and weapon training.
- **Wood Elf** — bonus to Wisdom, exceptional stealth in natural terrain, increased speed.
- **Drow (Dark Elf)** — bonus to Charisma, superior darkvision, innate minor spellcasting, but a
  vulnerability to sunlight after generations spent underground.

## Dwarf
Stout, hardy, and famed for craftsmanship, dwarves have darkvision, resistance to poison, and an innate
sense for stonework and masonry. Common subraces:
- **Hill Dwarf** — bonus to Wisdom, extra hit points per level.
- **Mountain Dwarf** — bonus to Strength, proficiency with armor.

## Halfling
Small, lucky, and famously hard to intimidate, halflings can reroll a natural 1 on key dice rolls and
move through the space of larger creatures with ease. Common subraces:
- **Lightfoot** — bonus to Charisma, able to hide behind creatures larger than themselves.
- **Stout** — bonus to Constitution, poison resistance.

## Dragonborn
Proud, draconic-blooded warriors, each dragonborn traces ancestry to a chromatic or metallic dragon
type and gains a breath weapon and damage resistance matching that ancestry (for example, a
red-dragon-descended dragonborn breathes fire and resists fire damage).

## Gnome
Small, inventive, and nearly impossible to affect with mind-influencing magic thanks to their
Gnome Cunning trait. Common subraces:
- **Forest Gnome** — bonus to Dexterity, minor illusion magic, can speak with small animals.
- **Rock Gnome** — bonus to Constitution, a knack for tinkering and inventing small devices.

## Half-Elf
Combining human adaptability with elven grace, half-elves get a Charisma bonus plus flexible bonuses to
two other abilities of the player's choice, along with elven traits like darkvision and resistance to
being charmed — often navigating between human and elven societies without fully belonging to either.

## Half-Orc
Powerful and relentless, half-orcs combine human versatility with orcish endurance: a bonus to Strength
and Constitution, darkvision, and the signature Relentless Endurance trait that lets them drop to 1 hit
point instead of 0 once per long rest rather than falling immediately.

## Tiefling
Bearing a trace of fiendish heritage (often several generations back), tieflings have darkvision,
resistance to fire damage, and minor innate spellcasting themed around darkness and fire, alongside a
bonus to Charisma and Intelligence.

## Size, speed, and languages
Most of these races are Medium size with a base walking speed of 30 feet; halflings and gnomes are
Small with a 25-foot speed. All start fluent in Common plus one or more racial languages (Elvish,
Dwarvish, Draconic, and so on), reflecting their people's history and homeland.
