# Spellcasting Basics

Spellcasting classes draw from a pool of **spell slots** that refresh (usually on a long rest) and are
spent to power spells of matching or lower level. A 3rd-level spell slot can cast a 3rd-level spell at
full strength, or a lower-level spell "upcast" for a stronger effect where the spell description allows.

## Spell levels

Spells range from **cantrips** (level 0 — castable at will, no slot required, that scale in power
purely with character level) up to 9th-level spells, the most powerful magic available, typically
accessible only to the highest-level casters.

## Casting time, range, and duration

Every spell specifies:
- **Casting time** — most spells take one action; some take a bonus action, a reaction (triggered
  spells like Shield), or much longer (ritual casting, or spells with a stated casting time of minutes
  or hours).
- **Range** — from Self or Touch out to hundreds of feet.
- **Duration** — instantaneous, a fixed time, or "until dispelled," with many ongoing effects requiring
  **concentration** to maintain.

## Components

Spells require some combination of:
- **Verbal (V)** — spoken words of power; impossible while silenced or gagged.
- **Somatic (S)** — precise hand gestures; impossible with hands full or bound.
- **Material (M)** — physical components, either mundane (consumed) or an arcane focus/component pouch
  that substitutes for cheaper, non-consumed materials.

## Concentration

Many ongoing spells require concentration — a caster can maintain only one concentration spell at a
time, and taking damage forces a Constitution saving throw (DC 10, or half the damage taken if higher)
to avoid losing the spell. Casting a new concentration spell automatically ends any previous one.

## Spell save DC and attack bonus

When a spell forces a target to resist with a saving throw, the DC is calculated as
`8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier`. When a spell instead requires an attack roll
(like most damaging touch or ranged spell attacks), the caster adds their proficiency bonus and
spellcasting ability modifier to the d20 roll, exactly like a weapon attack.

## Ritual casting

Spells tagged "ritual" can be cast without expending a spell slot by adding 10 minutes to the casting
time, available to any class whose feature explicitly allows ritual casting (classically wizards,
clerics, and druids).
