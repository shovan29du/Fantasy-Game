# Build Lesson Plan

Gather subject, age/grade, duration, standards or curriculum, class profile, language, available materials, and constraints. If details are missing, state reasonable assumptions.

Produce:

1. Measurable objectives using observable verbs.
2. Prerequisites and a quick diagnostic.
3. Timed sequence: hook, explicit teaching, guided practice, independent or group practice, and exit check.
4. Teacher actions, learner actions, questions to ask, and expected evidence of learning.
5. Differentiation for support, core, and extension groups.
6. A low-resource alternative and accessibility accommodations.
7. Formative assessment rubric, likely misconceptions, remediation, and homework or follow-up.

For multi-grade classrooms, organize a rotation so one group receives direct teaching while others complete safe independent work. Derived from the lesson-generation and rural multi-grade teaching ideas in PathShala AI and the supplied education platforms.
