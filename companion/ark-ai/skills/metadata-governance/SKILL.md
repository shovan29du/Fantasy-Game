# Metadata Governance

1. Inventory assets, domains, systems, owners, consumers, and critical decisions.
2. Give each asset a canonical identity and capture schema, description, ownership, freshness, usage, and certification.
3. Map table- and column-level lineage across ingestion, transformations, metrics, dashboards, ML, and applications.
4. Define glossary terms and metrics with grain, formula, dimensions, owner, and change policy.
5. Attach quality tests, incidents, policies, classifications, and contracts to governed assets.
6. Rank trusted, current, owned context in discovery and AI retrieval; expose provenance.
7. Define stewardship workflows and measurable coverage, freshness, test health, and adoption goals.

Never infer lineage or certification without evidence. Adapted from OpenMetadata's open-context-layer concepts; no OpenMetadata source is bundled.
